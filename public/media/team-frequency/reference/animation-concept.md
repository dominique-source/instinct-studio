# Animation concept

## Base motion

The section begins in near darkness. Portraits and film elements reveal in response to the visitor's scroll. The people (and the generic Art Directors slot) never enter as separate cards. Their visual fields overlap and share one connected signal.

**Update:** the shipped site presents three slots — Dominique, a generic

## Signal path

An electric-blue line travels through Dominique, the Art Directors slot and Neil. Its path reacts to scroll progress and briefly changes form near each slot: pulse, aperture and waveform.

## Signature interaction: The Shared Thought

Pointer movement creates a soft invisible influence field. Nearby fragments rotate and drift by a few pixels. When the pointer crosses a blue connection, a short thought pulse travels across the whole collage. The pulse reveals a different layer at each slot:

- Dominique: a raw handwritten question.
- Art Directors: the collaborators' mark brightens.
- Neil: one clear sentence.

The interaction shows one idea changing form through the studio's collaborators.

## Accessibility

The animation must have a reduced-motion version. Essential names, roles and meaning remain visible without movement.

# Animation concept

## Base motion

The section begins in near darkness. Portraits and film elements reveal in response to the visitor's scroll. The four people never enter as separate cards. Their visual fields overlap and share one connected signal.

## Signal path

An electric-blue line travels through Dominique, Stefan, Neil and Youri. Its path reacts to scroll progress and briefly changes form near each person: pulse, aperture, waveform and frame break.

## Signature interaction: The Shared Thought

Pointer movement creates a soft invisible influence field. Nearby fragments rotate and drift by a few pixels. When the pointer crosses a blue connection, a short thought pulse travels across the entire team. The pulse reveals a different layer at each person:

- Dominique: a raw handwritten question.
- Stefan: a film frame.
- Neil: one clear sentence.
- Youri: the frame breaks and recomposes.

The interaction shows one idea changing form through four minds.

## Accessibility

The animation must have a reduced-motion version. Essential names, roles and meaning remain visible without movement.
